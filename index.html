Key Areas of Focus

The key areas of focus for sustainability are transport, energy, and food.

True sustainability starts with understanding where you stand. Our system analyzes your daily lifestyle across the three core areas that matter most: your food choices, daily commute, and energy consumption. Your personalized eco-score is calculated directly from your answers in these three categories, giving you a clear starting point. Rather than expecting overwhelming change overnight, Sustainify breaks down your score to deliver tailored, high-impact micro-habits designed specifically for your routine. By taking simple, intentional steps in these key areas, you transform daily routines into measurable positive impact for the planet.

Each card below represents a key area of focus, and provides insights and tips to help you make more sustainable choices in your daily life.

🚲

Transport

How far and how you travel each day, whether by bike, car, bus, or bicycle, has a major impact on your carbon footprint.

View tips ↓
  • Walk, cycle, or use public transport for short journeys.
  • Combine errands into one trip and carpool when possible.
  • Keep your tires properly inflated to improve fuel efficiency.
💡

Energy

How much you use air conditioning and lights at home: small habits can make a big difference to energy use.

View tips ↓
  • Switch to LED bulbs and turn lights off in empty rooms.
  • Use natural light and air whenever the weather allows.
  • Unplug chargers and devices when they are not in use.
🍽️

Food

Plastic use and food waste are everyday lifestyle choices that directly affect the environment.

View tips ↓
  • Plan portions before cooking to prevent food waste.
  • Choose reusable bottles, bags, and containers instead of single-use plastic.
  • Buy local, seasonal ingredients when they are available.
